# apedin

**Follow the trades. Keep the context.**

apedin brings observed Pump trader activity and community discussion into one place, with source attribution and context for the activity you see.

[Open apedin](https://apedin.family/) · [Follow on X](https://x.com/apedinfamily) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md) · [Transparency](TRANSPARENCY.md) · [Security](SECURITY.md)

## What you can do

- Browse observed Pump buys and sells from a watchlist combining the top 100 realized-profit accounts across daily, weekly and monthly leaderboard snapshots.
- Use separate Buys and Sells checkboxes, both enabled initially. Other source feeds and historical callouts are hidden from the main feed.
- See available dated ranks, coin age, and brief market-cap and holder-count observations with sparklines.
- Explore token rooms, caller profiles and source-linked activity histories.
- Browse the [trader directory](https://apedin.family/traders) and open supported public profiles with recorded activity, account-specific totals and dated social and trading-account links. Connection evidence is shown separately from ownership verification.
- Sign in to reply and participate in discussions.
- Follow callers and use filters to focus your feed.
- Explore activity and experimental Attention indicators, with missing-data and coverage limitations shown where available.

Source availability and history vary. Imported activity is attributed to its source; an imported profile does not establish that someone has claimed or controls it. See [how we describe our data](TRANSPARENCY.md).

## Keep the context

Observed trades, historical callouts, rankings, Attention indicators, badges and XP are not investment recommendations or assurances about a token, a caller or future performance. The service does not execute trades or take custody of funds. Account features are for adults aged 18 and over.
