# apedin

**Real people. Real calls.**

apedin brings meme-coin callouts and community discussion into one place, with source attribution and context for the activity you see.

[Open apedin](https://apedin.family/) · [Follow on X](https://x.com/apedinfamily) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md) · [Transparency](TRANSPARENCY.md) · [Security](SECURITY.md)

## What you can do

- Browse callouts from supported sources including Pump, FOMO, GMGN and Axiom.
- Explore token rooms, caller profiles and source-linked activity histories.
- Browse the [trader directory](https://apedin.family/traders) and open supported public profiles with recorded activity, account-specific totals and dated social and trading-account links. Connection evidence is shown separately from ownership verification.
- Sign in to post calls, reply and participate in discussions.
- Follow callers and use filters to focus your feed.
- See which other currently ranked/KOL source accounts mentioned a token, with a latest-mention list from captured history.
- Explore activity and experimental Attention indicators, with missing-data and coverage limitations shown where available.
- Review experimental Observations that summarise caller market-cap habits and overlapping community participation from the recorded dataset.

Source availability and history vary. Imported activity is attributed to its source; an imported profile does not establish that someone has claimed or controls it. See [how we describe our data](TRANSPARENCY.md).

## Feedback and support

For a reproducible product bug or feature suggestion, email [apedinfamily@outlook.com](mailto:apedinfamily@outlook.com). Include the page, what you expected, what happened and your browser/device where relevant.

Report suspected vulnerabilities **privately**, following our [security policy](SECURITY.md). Account, privacy and content-removal requests should also go through email rather than a public discussion.

## Keep the context

Callouts, rankings, Attention indicators, badges and XP are not investment recommendations or assurances about a token, a caller or future performance. The service does not execute trades or take custody of funds. Account features are for adults aged 18 and over.
